Meet Erumi

Welcome to Erumi!

Erumi is an open-source, cinema-grade anime streaming platform designed to give you an ad-free, ultra-responsive watching experience powered by the Yorumi backend engine.

100% Ad-Free & Private Zero trackers, zero popups, zero sponsored interruptions.
Multi-Device LAN Stream Stream seamlessly to your Phone, Tablet, or TV on the same Wi-Fi.
Fast HLS Direct Play Instant CDN proxy bypass with sub/dub and MPV player support.
AniList Metadata High-resolution posters, synopses, and community ratings.
